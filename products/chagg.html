How does it work?

Chagg 4UL - is a single virtual network channel at the packet level over multiple cellular networks.
Like most WMT (Wireless Multiplex Technology) systems - Chagg 4UL is available for Internet access. Chagg 4UL uses the binded bandwidth of all modems together providing enormous upload speeds. The device also supports full databinding across all connections and is available via the Wi-Fi hotspot or via the Ethernet connection.
We work with modems using 3G (UMTS), EDGE, GPRS, GSM, HSPA+, LTE. And with any IP channels (WiFi, Ethernet, Fiber optic, radio …).
