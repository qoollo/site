JUNGLEFOX
right here and now

JungleFox is a platform for searching and creating events as well as actions.

Basic Functionality:

  • search for various events around the world, with reference to the subject, location and time interval;
  • the possibility of creating your own event;
  • search for shares held around;
  • the opportunity to post information about your business.

Using the bundle of Angular + RubyOnRails made it possible to completely satisfy the customer's requirements, to develop a convenient and fast-configurable interface for searching and viewing events, as well as a flexible interface for the editors of the articles.
