INSUREYE
Insureye's Platform provides insights into Canadian insurance market

Insureye’s platform collects and analyzes statistical data on different types of insurance products from different insurers in Canada. There are four free services: Credit Card Navigator, InsurEye Price Comparison, InsurEye Price Comparsion, Insurance Reviews and Consumer testimonials.

Insureye's Tools

Credit Card Navigator

Interactive tool for exploring Credit Card's features along side with prices.

Price comparison

The Tool allows you to compare prices and rates of insurers. All statistics are presented with the help of graphs, displayed according to different criteria: gender, age and residence.

Consumer reviews

You can choose one type of insurance product and look through reviews procided by customer all over Canada:

  • Mortgage
  • Credit Card
  • Auto insurance
  • Home
  • Motorcycle
  • Life

Consumer testimonials

If you need to collect a feedback from customers and quickly sort and present them in an accessible form, this widget will help you. Just share a link with your customer. User will be able to leave his testimonial in a couple of clicks.
